# Methodology and evidence boundaries

The implementation specification is the root NYC311_BigData_Codex_Implementation_Plan.md. The original proposal was described by the user; its PDF was not independently provided in this repository.

Official provenance: selected NYC Open Data erm2-nwe9 fields, daily windows, stable keyset pagination, deterministic ID-suffix17 sample, optional token and bounded retry. Approximate suffix fraction is not a demonstrated sampling probability. Date coverage completeness concerns the selected sample, not the entire city. Snapshot field revisions cannot be reconstructed. Floating times use America/New_York: earlier offset at autumn ambiguity, spring gaps quarantined, UTC silver storage.

Quality denominator: input rows = rejected invalid rows + duplicate valid snapshots + accepted unique requests. Latest valid observation wins with deterministic content hash on ties. Quarantine preserves rejected reasons; invalid boroughs become UNKNOWN and are counted.

Survival: closure timestamps, not final status, define observed events. Fixed as_of truncates future closures and right-censors open requests. Zero durations are floored to one minute and counted. KM group summaries expose sample sizes and censoring. A missing median is not imputed. Cox uses training-only vocabularies, penalization and creation calendar/categories; label cutoffs differ correctly between training and holdout. Agency may reflect later routing, a source limitation. Harrell concordance is censor-aware but may be sensitive to censoring distribution. PH diagnostics are descriptive; rejection is disclosed. Holdout scores/labels permit recomputation. Baseline group medians and common-horizon survival scores are identified separately, retaining missing-median denominators.

Streaming: created/closed history only; synthetic update/disorder/duplicate/late flags identify interventions. At-least-once Spark foreachBatch is made business-idempotent with a durable unique event ledger, monotone versions and a transaction containing state, prediction and batch marker. Database-commit-before-checkpoint failure is tested on real PostgreSQL. Pipeline/checkpoint identity prevents stale batch-ID collisions. Persistent ledger windows accept late corrections; no finite watermark completeness claim is made. Synthetic and official demos use separate schemas.

Weather: ERA5 gridded historical reanalysis at one point approximates NYC, with local dates. Complaints and weather association uses only complete observed sample days, controls month where estimable and cannot establish causality. Three months cannot demonstrate a full annual cycle; weekday/spring transition findings are pilot observations.

Capacity: cohort-only FIFO queue, closure counts as daily capacity proxy, no external backlog, homogeneous difficulty, proportional capacity uplift and fixed arrivals. Compare observed KM median to simulated baseline. Failed/unreached calibration suppresses precise deltas; even successful in-sample calibration is weak evidence and not a staffing causal estimate. Include agency/borough sample thresholds and complaint composition.

Benchmarks: same Spark ETL, fixed partitions, deterministic bounded official base. Isolated unique-ID replicas are explicitly synthetic. Single-host standalone actual Worker/executor processes are distinguished from local thread mode. Record resource differences, startup time, workload time, attempt status and complete repetition medians. Failed denominators persist. No cross-host or production throughput claim.
